The Spotify mark in `spotify.svg` is the original Simple Icons shape distributed
as `SiSpotify` in the existing Playlist Port `react-icons/si` dependency.
It is used with Spotify green (#1DB954); the mark itself is unmodified.

Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/spotify.svg
Distribution: https://github.com/react-icons/react-icons (Simple Icons set)
Simple Icons license: CC0-1.0.

The transfer arrows and playlist glyph on the cover are separate Material UI icons.
